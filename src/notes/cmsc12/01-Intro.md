## Introduction to Computer Science

### What is Compsci?
- Computer science isn't just coding encoding, or game development.
- It's also a very wide field.

To understand what **compsci** is, let's look at what **computing** is.
According to the **Association of Computing Machinery** (ACM):
- **Computing** is any goal-oriented activity requiring, benefiting from, or creating computers.
- **Computing** is designing and building hardware and software systems for a wide range of applications.
- **Computing** is processing, structuring, and managing various types of information.
- **Computing** is doing scientific studies using computers.
- **Computing** is making computers behave intelligently.

**Computing Disciplines** include:
- Computer Engineering
- Computer Science
- Information Systems
- Information Technology
- Software Engineering

Now, back to the original question.
According to the **ACM**, **Computer Science** is:
- the study of problems,
- design and analysis of efficient algorithms and data structures for their solutions,
- programming languages for implementing DSA,
- physical hardware for running the programs,
- software systems for efficient use of resources,
- and software engineering methodologies to manage large software projects.

What do **Computer Scientists** do?
- They design and implement software.
- They devise new ways of using computers.
- They develop effective ways to solve computing problems.
